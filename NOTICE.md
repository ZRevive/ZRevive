# ZRevive — what this is, and what it is not

ZRevive is a **free, non-commercial fan project**. It brings back *H1Z1: King of the Kill*
Preseason 5 gameplay on community-run servers, for people who already own *Z1 Battle Royale*
on Steam.

## Nobody makes money from this

- There is **nothing to buy**. No shop, no premium accounts, no paid ranks, no paid cosmetics.
- **No donations**, no Patreon, no tips, no "supporter" perks.
- **No advertising**, no affiliate links, no sponsorships.
- **No cryptocurrency**, no NFTs, no resale of anything.
- **Crowns**, the in-game currency, are earned **only by playing**. They cannot be bought with
  real money, and they cannot be cashed out. Trading is between players, for in-game items only.

If you are ever asked to pay for ZRevive, by anyone, it is not us.

## We do not distribute Daybreak's game

*H1Z1*, *King of the Kill* and *Z1 Battle Royale* — and all of the art, models, textures,
sounds, animations and game data in them — belong to **Daybreak Game Company LLC**. ZRevive is
not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Daybreak.

**We ship none of their files.** This is a deliberate design decision, not a formality:

- The launcher reads the copy of *Z1 Battle Royale* **you already own on Steam** and builds a
  separate ZRevive folder from it, on your own PC. Your Steam install is never modified.
- Our updates are a **patch recipe**, not a copy. Where we change a game data sheet, the
  launcher reads the original rows out of your own install and rewrites them locally. The
  bytes never travel over the network.
- The only files we distribute are ones **we made ourselves** — our server, our launcher, our
  own textures and cosmetics.

If you do not own *Z1 Battle Royale*, ZRevive will not build, and that is on purpose.
